Cosmetic And Restorative Dentistry

Dental crowns crafted and fitted with the highest level of care.

A dental crown is custom-made to fit over a damaged natural tooth. When a tooth has lost too much strength or structure to be reliably repaired with a simple filling, a crown can restore function, protection and appearance.

What is a crown?

A dental crown fits over the existing tooth structure like a protective cap. At Elite Dentistry, crowns are used where a tooth needs reinforcement, improved durability and a more complete restoration of shape and appearance.

Porcelain crowns can be matched closely to the shade and translucency of surrounding teeth, which makes them a strong choice when aesthetics matter. Zirconia crowns offer even greater strength and are often better suited to teeth further back in the mouth where heavy chewing forces are involved.

When do you need a dental crown?

Dental crowns have several important uses in restorative and cosmetic dentistry. They are commonly recommended when a tooth is too compromised for a smaller repair to last well over time.

  • Severely decayed teeth that have lost too much structure
  • Teeth weakened after root canal treatment
  • Fractured, broken or heavily worn teeth
  • Teeth with failing or very large fillings
  • Situations where strength and appearance need to be restored together

What does the treatment involve?

A dental crown is usually completed over two or three appointments. The first step is a clinical examination so the tooth can be assessed properly and the most suitable restoration can be recommended.

After the tooth is prepared, impressions or digital records are taken so the crown can be fabricated precisely for your bite, tooth shape and surrounding smile line. A temporary crown is typically placed while the final restoration is being made.

Once the crown is ready, you return for a fitting appointment. The finished restoration is checked for fit, comfort and appearance, then adjusted where needed before it is bonded securely into place.

Looking after your crown

Although a crown is a durable restoration, it still needs proper care. Good oral hygiene, sensible chewing habits and regular check-ups help protect the tooth underneath and extend the life of the restoration.

Clean thoroughly

Brush and clean carefully around the crown margin to reduce plaque build-up and protect gum health.

Protect the restoration

Avoid habits like chewing ice or using teeth as tools, which can damage even strong restorative work.

Attend reviews

Regular examinations help identify bite issues, wear or gum changes before they become bigger problems.

Plan the full pathway

If the crown is part of larger restorative work, follow the recommended sequence so the final result performs well long-term.
