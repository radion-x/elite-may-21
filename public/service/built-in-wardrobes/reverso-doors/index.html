The Reverso® Concept

Where Built-In Becomes Walk-In

The Reverso® mechanism is Presotto’s exclusive patented system, engineered specifically for the Kabinarmadio wardrobe. Unlike a traditional hinged door, Reverso® doors pivot on a precision axis that swings the door panel fully aside — opening the entire interior with a single gesture.

The result: no drawer units blocking the interior, no partition panels dividing the space. Just a single, uninterrupted volume where everything is simultaneously visible and within arm’s reach — the practicality of a walk-in closet contained within a built-in footprint.

Interiors

A Walk-In Experience in a Built-In Footprint

Because the Reverso® doors swing completely clear of the interior, there is no need for drawer units or partitions that typically block access. The full width — from 72cm to 197cm — opens in a single movement.

We configure each Kabinarmadio interior to your precise habits: full-length hanging, double-hang for shirts and jackets, pull-out shoe drawers, rotating carousels for bags and accessories, and jewellery drawers with magnetic dividers — all illuminated by integrated LED lighting.

Reverso® Pivot Mechanism

Presotto’s patented pivot system swings the door completely clear of the cabinet opening. No track, no overhead rail — just a clean axis and precision engineering.

Three Door Finishes

Partition-Free Interior
