Gum Health

Protect the foundation around your teeth.

Healthy gums are essential to long-term oral health. This page supports patients who are dealing with inflammation, bleeding gums, maintenance issues or signs of periodontal disease.

When this page is relevant

  • Bleeding, tenderness or swollen gums
  • Deep cleaning or maintenance support
  • Monitoring gum stability around existing teeth or restorations
