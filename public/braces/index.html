Orthodontic Alternative

Braces remain a useful option where fixed treatment is the better clinical choice.

This page gives patients a clear destination when aligners are not the only option worth considering.

Braces may be recommended depending on the complexity of movement required, the predictability needed and the most suitable path for long-term alignment.
