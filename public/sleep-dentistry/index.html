Comfort Pathways

More support for patients who feel anxious or need a calmer treatment experience.

Sleep dentistry is not only about the procedure. It starts with understanding anxiety levels, treatment complexity and what kind of support will help you feel more in control.

Who this may help

  • Patients with significant dental anxiety
  • Patients having longer or more involved treatment
  • Patients who have delayed care because the experience feels overwhelming
