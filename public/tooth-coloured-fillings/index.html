Discreet Repair

Restore a tooth while keeping the repair natural looking.

Tooth coloured fillings are designed to repair decay or minor damage without drawing attention to the treatment.

  • Repair decay or chips
  • Preserve more natural tooth structure where appropriate
  • Blend the restoration more comfortably into the smile
