Wisdom Teeth

Assessment and removal when wisdom teeth become difficult to manage.

Pain, crowding, partial eruption or infection around wisdom teeth can create ongoing problems. Elite Dentistry helps assess whether removal is the right pathway.

Reasons patients usually inquire

  • Pain, pressure or infection around back teeth
  • Difficulty cleaning or food trapping
  • Advice after x-rays or referral
