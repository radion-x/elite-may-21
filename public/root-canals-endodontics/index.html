Save The Tooth

Treat infection inside the tooth while preserving the tooth itself.

Root canal treatment is often the option patients ask about when a tooth is infected or increasingly painful but still worth saving.

  • Assess whether the tooth can be predictably saved
  • Treat infection inside the tooth structure
  • Plan the restoration afterward, often with a crown when needed
