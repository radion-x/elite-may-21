Patient Forms

Everything you need before your appointment.

Use this page to understand what to bring, what to expect and how to contact the clinic if you need help before your visit.

Before your first appointment

  • Bring any relevant dental history, referral information or recent x-rays if you have them.
  • Allow enough time to arrive calmly and discuss your goals or concerns in full.
  • If you feel anxious, let the team know early so the appointment can be tailored appropriately.

Need a callback instead?

If you are reviewing treatment options after hours, leave your details and the clinic will be in touch during business hours.
