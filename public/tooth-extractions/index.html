Tooth Removal

Remove a tooth carefully, with a clear plan for what comes next.

Extractions are recommended only when a tooth cannot be predictably saved or is creating wider oral health issues. The next-step replacement or healing plan matters just as much as the removal itself.

  • Remove damaged or unsalvageable teeth
  • Manage pain, infection or structural compromise
  • Discuss replacement pathways such as implants or bridges
