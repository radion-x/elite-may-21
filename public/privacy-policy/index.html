Privacy

How Elite Dentistry handles personal information.

This page outlines the information the website collects, why it is collected and how it is used in connection with enquiries, bookings and clinic communication.

Information collected

Website forms may collect your name, email address, phone number, treatment interest and the content of your message so the clinic can respond appropriately.

How information is used

The information is used to respond to enquiries, arrange appointments, follow up on callback requests and improve the patient enquiry process.

Storage and disclosure

Enquiry information may be stored within the site's systems and shared with clinic staff who need it to respond to your request. It is not sold to third parties.

Contact

If you have questions about website privacy or the way your details are handled, contact Elite Dentistry directly via the clinic phone number or email address listed on the contact page.
