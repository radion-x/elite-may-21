Regeneration Support

Additional support for more complex bone and soft tissue needs.

This page exists for patients whose implant planning involves more advanced regeneration support to create a healthier long-term result.

Guided bone and tissue regeneration may be discussed where both hard and soft tissue support are important to implant stability, healing and aesthetics.
