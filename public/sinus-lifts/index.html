Upper Implant Planning

Create the height needed for selected upper implant cases.

A sinus lift may be recommended where bone height in the upper jaw needs improvement before implant placement can proceed predictably.

This page helps patients understand why additional upper-jaw preparation may be part of implant planning and how it fits into the overall sequence.
