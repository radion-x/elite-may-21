Urgent Care

Prompt support for pain, damage, swelling and urgent dental concerns.

If the issue feels urgent, call the clinic directly. This page helps patients understand what counts as urgent and what the team will focus on first.

Common emergency reasons

  • Toothache, swelling or signs of infection
  • Broken, chipped or knocked teeth
  • Lost restorations or sudden sensitivity
  • Bleeding or trauma affecting the mouth

If you are reaching out after hours, use the contact page and explain the issue clearly so the clinic can assess urgency when it reopens.
