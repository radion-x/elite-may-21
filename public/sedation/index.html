Sedation Support

Sedation is considered when more support would improve comfort and confidence.

Suitability depends on your health history, the treatment involved and what level of support the clinical team believes is appropriate.

What patients usually want to know

  • Whether sedation is appropriate for their level of anxiety
  • How treatment planning changes when sedation is part of the appointment
  • What preparation and aftercare may be required
