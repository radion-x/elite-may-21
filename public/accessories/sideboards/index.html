The Madie Collection

Storage as Architecture

Presotto’s Madie collection approaches the sideboard not as furniture, but as architecture. Each piece is designed with the proportional rigour and material honesty that defines Italian modernism — slim profiles, continuous surfaces, hardware that disappears into the form.

Available in the full range of Presotto lacquers, veneers, and metal finishes, each sideboard can be configured to align precisely with the material palette of your built-in cabinetry — creating a cohesive interior language throughout your home.

Design Detail

Inclineart — The Sculptural Sideboard

The Inclineart model introduces an angled door geometry that transforms a functional piece into a room-defining sculpture. The inclined plane catches light differently throughout the day — creating depth and interest without ornament.

Available in lacquer, veneer or a combination of both — with the same push-to-open mechanism used in Presotto’s cabinetry range. No handles, no interruptions to the surface.

Specification

Italian Craft, Sydney Delivered

Full Lacquer Range

Over 60 lacquer colours available including all matte, gloss and soft-touch variants. Matched to Presotto’s cabinetry palette for complete interior consistency.

Natural Veneer Options

Selected European oak, walnut, and ash veneers. Finished with natural oils or clear lacquer to preserve the authentic grain character of each species.

Modular Sizing

Select models support custom widths and module combinations — allowing the sideboard to be configured precisely for your wall dimension and proportion.

Metal Base Options

Brushed stainless, black powder-coat or champagne anodised aluminium legs available across the range — complementing both warm and cool interior palettes.

Push-to-Open Mechanism

Handle-free door systems with soft-close integrated mechanisms. The same hardware philosophy as Presotto’s cabinetry — clean surfaces, invisible function.

White-Glove Delivery

All pieces are delivered and installed by our team in Sydney. No flat-pack assembly, no couriers — every piece arrives ready to position and styled to your specification.
