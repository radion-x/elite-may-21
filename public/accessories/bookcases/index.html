The Tower Collection

Structure as Statement

The Tower bookcase by Presotto is an exercise in structural honesty. Open shelving modules in lacquer and veneer rise with quiet authority — every joint visible, every proportion deliberate. It is furniture that reveals its making.

Available as a standalone unit or configured across an entire wall, Tower adapts to any room dimension. Closed modules at the base provide concealed storage while open shelves above remain light and accessible.

Design Detail

Open Display, Concealed Order

The hinged-door module within the Tower system offers the best of both approaches — an open display face that closes to a flush, handleless panel at a touch. The interior is configured to your storage requirements: shelves, drawers, or a combination.

The same push-to-open mechanism used in our wardrobes and joinery ensures the door disappears into the composition when closed — no visible hardware, no interruption to the surface.
