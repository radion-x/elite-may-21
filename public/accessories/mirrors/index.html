The Specchio Collection

A Mirror Is a Room’s Second Window

A mirror does more than reflect. Positioned correctly, it doubles the perceived depth of a room, amplifies light from a distant window, and creates a sense of space where there was previously only surface. Presotto’s Specchio mirrors are designed with this spatial intelligence in mind.

Available in three forms — the framed precision of Mimesi, the porthole presence of Oblo, and the full-wall immersion of Tuttospecchio — each mirror is available in Presotto’s metal and lacquer frame finishes, coordinated with your cabinetry palette.

Design Detail

Mimesi — The Framed Field

The Mimesi mirror is Presotto’s most versatile mirror design: a rectangular field within a precision metal or lacquered frame that can be scaled to any wall dimension. Horizontal or vertical orientation, wall or floor-leaning — the same object, different postures.

Available in brushed stainless, black or champagne anodised aluminium frames — in all widths from a bedside aperture to a full dressing-room wall. Coordinated with the metal finishes across your wardrobe and hardware selections.

Specification

Italian Craft, Sydney Delivered

Mimesi — The Framed Rectangle

A precision metal or lacquered frame around a true-flat mirror surface. Scalable to any wall dimension, available in horizontal or vertical orientation.

Oblo — The Circular

A porthole mirror for the entry or living room: circular form with a lacquered or metal rim. A considered counterpoint to the rectilinear geometry of contemporary cabinetry.

Tuttospecchio — Full Height

A floor-to-ceiling frameless glass panel that transforms a wall into a reflective surface. The dressing room standard: no frame, no border, just light and depth.

Metal Frame Finishes

Brushed stainless, black powder-coat and champagne anodised aluminium frames available across the range — matched to the hardware throughout your interior.

Lacquer Frame Options

Lacquered frames in all 60+ Presotto colours — coordinated with wardrobe, sideboard or joinery cabinetry for a coherent interior finish palette.

Professional Installation

Large-format mirrors are installed by our team with specialist fixings calibrated to the wall construction. No visible fixings, perfectly plumb — finished to the standard of the cabinetry it accompanies.
