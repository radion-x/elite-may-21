Benches & Ottomans

The Piece That Completes the Room

A bench at the foot of the bed is one of the most considered decisions in a bedroom. It must be the right height, the right depth, and the right material weight — light enough not to dominate, present enough to anchor. Presotto’s Cali, Sigmund and Dormeuse benches are designed with exactly this precision.

The pouf range — Dama, Drop, Pilar and Quadrato — offers more flexible placement: beside an armchair in the living room, at the base of a dressing table, or as a standalone object in a generous entry. Each is available in the full Presotto upholstery library.

Design Detail

Sigmund — The Bedroom Anchor

The Sigmund bench is scaled for the bedroom: a generous seat depth, a height that aligns with the mattress, upholstered in fabric that complements rather than competes with your wardrobe finishes.

Available in straight and curved configurations to suit different room layouts, Sigmund can be specified in any Presotto fabric or leather — with the option to match an armchair or dressing table stool for a fully coordinated bedroom suite.
