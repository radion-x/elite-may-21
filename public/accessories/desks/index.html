A Workspace Worth Keeping

The desk is the study’s defining object. It sets the scale of the room, frames the view, and determines how one sits within the space. Presotto’s Scrittoi writing desks approach this responsibility with the rigour that defines Italian furniture design — every proportion deliberate, every surface considered.

From the Releve with its elevated signature to the Bloom’s curved form, the range spans six distinct approaches to the writing surface. Every desk is available in Presotto’s lacquers, veneers and metal finishes — coordinated with the built-in cabinetry in your home office or study.

Design Detail

Releve — The Suspended Workspace

The Releve desk echoes the design logic of Presotto’s Releve coffee table: a writing surface elevated on a metal column, creating a desk that appears to float above the floor. The result is a study object of remarkable visual lightness — present without weight.

Available in all Presotto lacquers with column options in brushed stainless, black or champagne aluminium. A cable management channel runs through the column for a completely clean desk surface without visible cabling.

Full Lacquer Range

Desk surfaces in all 60+ Presotto lacquers — matte, gloss and soft-touch. Coordinated with home office cabinetry for a unified workspace palette.

Natural Veneer Surfaces

Oak, walnut and ash veneer writing surfaces available across select models — oil-finished or clear-lacquered for a workspace that improves with age.

Integrated Storage

Select models include cable channels, drawer units and side return configurations — allowing the desk to function as both workspace and storage within a single clean form.

Cable Management

Integrated cable routing channels in Releve and Gulliver models keep desk surfaces free of cabling — a workspace that looks as resolved when in use as when cleared.

Mobile Caddy

The Caddy wheeled desk can be moved room-to-room: positioned at a window during the day, rolled against a wall when the space is reclaimed for other uses in the evening.

White-Glove Installation

All fixed desks are delivered, assembled and wired for cable management by our team. Mobile desks are assembled and positioned. Final sign-off includes a demonstration of all integrated features.
