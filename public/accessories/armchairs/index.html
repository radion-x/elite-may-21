A Chair as a Room’s Punctuation

An armchair is the piece a room’s occupant chooses deliberately. It signals a position, a preference, a commitment to comfort over utility. Presotto’s small armchair collection offers that commitment in four distinct forms — each one conceived in Italy and available to order through our Double Bay studio.

Every model is available in the full range of Presotto upholstery fabrics and leathers — the same material library used across their cabinetry range, ensuring your armchair can be coordinated with your wardrobe or joinery commission to a single colour reference.

Design Detail

Milo — The Considered Companion

The Milo armchair is designed for rooms where a single chair makes a statement. Its low, wide silhouette suits contemporary environments where seating is low to the floor — a modernist proportion that remains comfortable for extended reading or conversation.

Specify fabric from the full Presotto upholstery library — bouclé, velvet, linen, technical leather — in any of the standard colourways or request a custom colour from our studio. The chair frame is available in natural oak or lacquered white.

Specification

Italian Craft, Sydney Delivered

Full Upholstery Library

Over 80 fabric options including bouclé, velvet, linen weaves, and technical leathers. All coordinated with Presotto’s cabinetry colour palette.

Metal Leg Options

Brushed stainless, black powder-coat, or champagne anodised aluminium legs where applicable. Matched to the metal finishes available across the Presotto range.

Wood Frame Variants

Natural oak or lacquered frames available across select models — in all Presotto standard lacquer colours for complete material continuity.

Bedroom Integration

The Mimosa small armchair is scaled specifically for dressing rooms and bedrooms — compact enough to accompany a wardrobe without dominating the space.

Made in Italy

Each chair is hand-upholstered in Pordenone, northeast Italy. The same production atelier that builds every Presotto wardrobe, sideboard and bookcase — one quality standard.

White-Glove Delivery

All pieces are delivered and placed by our team in Sydney. Fully assembled and positioned to your specification — no self-assembly required.
