Occasional Tables

The Living Room’s Centre of Gravity

A coffee table is the living room’s most negotiated object. It must be low enough to be unobtrusive, dimensional enough to be functional, and composed enough to anchor the seating arrangement without competing with it. Presotto’s tavolino range approaches this challenge with the precision that defines Italian furniture design.

With thirteen distinct models — from the elevated Releve to the low geometric Darwin — the range offers a form for every contemporary interior. Each table is available in Presotto’s lacquers, veneers, glass and metal finishes.

Design Detail

Releve — The Elevated Table

The Releve table is Presotto’s most architectural coffee table: a slim lacquered top elevated on a single metal column, creating a piece that feels suspended above the floor. The table top floats, the room breathes beneath it.

Available in all Presotto lacquer colours with a choice of column finishes in brushed stainless, black or champagne aluminium. Select models support a glass top option for additional visual lightness.

Lacquer Finish Range

Table tops in all 60+ Presotto lacquers — matte, gloss and soft-touch. Coordinated with cabinetry and sideboard finishes for a unified interior palette.

Glass Surfaces

Clear, smoked and extra-white glass top options across select models — adding visual lightness and reflecting the room’s quality of light.

Stone-Effect Tops

Select models feature Presotto’s stone-effect finish — a durable mineral-textured surface that evokes marble without its maintenance demands.

Metal Base Options

Brushed stainless, black powder-coat or champagne anodised aluminium column and leg options — matched to hardware throughout your interior.

Natural Veneer

Oak, walnut and ash veneer table tops available across the range — oil-finished or clear-lacquered to preserve the natural grain character.
