The Room Beyond the Wardrobe

A wardrobe does not exist in isolation. The sideboards that anchor a living room, the desk that defines a study, the mirror that frames an entry — these are the pieces that complete the architecture of a home.

As the exclusive Sydney partner for Presotto Italia, we extend our collection beyond cabinetry to the full range of Presotto’s furniture pieces. Every item can be sourced alongside your wardrobe or joinery commission — ensuring your entire interior shares a single design language.
